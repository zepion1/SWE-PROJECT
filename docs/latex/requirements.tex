\section{1. Introduction}\label{introduction}

\subsection{1.1. Purpose}\label{purpose}

This document defines the functional and non-functional requirements of the AI-Driven Pharmaceutical Real-Time Inventory and Customer Information System, along with high-level specifications describing its expected behavior. It serves as a reference for the development team and stakeholders throughout system design, implementation, and testing, ensuring a shared understanding of the system\textquotesingle s intended functionality and constraints.

\subsection{1.2. Scope}\label{scope}

The system will cover inventory management operations for participating pharmacies, including product and supplier records, stock tracking, expiration management, purchase orders, and reporting. It will also include AI-based demand forecasting and inventory recommendations, as well as a customer-facing portal for accessing medication availability, pricing, and pharmacy locations.

The system will support inventory updates based on sales recorded at the pharmacy\textquotesingle s point-of-sale system. Inventory decisions involving stock adjustments and purchase orders will remain subject to approval by authorized pharmacy personnel.

Inventory will be tracked by pharmacy, product, and lot. Staff will enter delivery counts by hand. Barcode scanning, separate stock balances for warehouses or rooms within a pharmacy, and stock transfers between locations are outside the project scope. The customer portal will provide a distance-sorted list of pharmacies; an embedded map will be deferred.

Also out of scope for this version are prescription dispensing and e-prescribing, insurance claim processing, online payment, home delivery, and any medical or dosing advice.

\section{2. Overall Description}\label{overall-description}

\subsection{2.1. System Overview}\label{system-overview}

The system will operate as a centralized platform connecting multiple participating pharmacies through a shared application. Each pharmacy will maintain its own inventory records, while the platform will provide a unified way to access and manage information across locations.

The application will have two main views: one for pharmacy operations and another for customers. The pharmacy view will provide role-based access to operational functions, while the customer view will allow public access to product information with optional account registration for additional features.

Inventory information will be updated through staff actions and sales recorded at the pharmacy\textquotesingle s point-of-sale system. Machine learning models will analyze historical sales data to generate recommendations for pharmacy personnel to review.

The system will distinguish between staff roles, customers, and system administrators, with their respective responsibilities and permissions described in \textbf{Table 2.1.1.}

\subsubsection*{UC-01. Receive stock}
\textbf{Actors:} Pharmacy Technician, Pharmacist / Manager.

\textbf{Requirements:} F3, F5, F6, F7, F14.

\textbf{Receive stock: basic course of events}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The technician opens the purchase order for the delivery.
\item The system will display the ordered products and quantities and the linked purchase invoice.
\item The technician enters each delivered package's NDC, lot number, expiration date and physical count.
\item The system will compare the entries with the purchase order and purchase invoice.
\item The technician confirms that all displayed values match.
\item The system will record a correct delivery, generate a GRN containing the confirmed physical quantities, link it to the purchase order and purchase invoice, and update stock.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Receive stock -- extra product}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The technician opens the purchase order for the delivery.
\item The system will display the ordered products and quantities and the linked purchase invoice.
\item The technician selects Record unlisted package and enters the NDC, lot number, expiration date and physical count printed on or counted from the delivered package.
\item The system will find no purchase-order line containing the entered NDC and display The purchase order does not list this product, together with Edit entry and Confirm extra product actions.
\item The technician selects Confirm extra product.
\item The system will record an extra-product discrepancy and an incorrect delivery.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Receive stock -- missing product or lot}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The technician opens the purchase order for the delivery.
\item The system will display the ordered products and quantities and the linked purchase invoice.
\item The technician selects the expected product and lot that were not delivered and enters a physical count of zero.
\item The system will display the ordered quantity and entered physical count of zero, together with Edit count and Confirm zero actions.
\item The technician selects Confirm zero.
\item The system will record a missing-product-or-lot discrepancy and an incorrect delivery.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Receive stock -- delivered product is expired}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The technician opens the purchase order for the delivery.
\item The system will display the ordered products and quantities and the linked purchase invoice.
\item The technician enters the NDC, lot number, expiration date and physical count printed on or counted from the delivered package.
\item The system will determine that the entered expiration date is before the delivery date and will display Edit entry and Confirm expired product actions.
\item The technician selects Confirm expired product.
\item The system will record an expired-product discrepancy and an incorrect delivery.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Receive stock -- technician confirms a quantity difference}
\begin{enumerate}
\item The technician opens the purchase order for the delivery.
\item The system will display the ordered products and quantities and the linked purchase invoice.
\item The technician enters a positive physical count that differs from the expected quantity.
\item The system will display the expected quantity and physical count, together with Edit count and Confirm entered count actions.
\item The technician recounts the units. The technician may select Edit count, enter another physical count and recount again any number of times.
\item The technician selects Confirm entered count while the physical count differs from the expected quantity.
\item The system will record a quantity discrepancy and an incorrect delivery.
\end{enumerate}

\textbf{Completion of an incorrect delivery.} The Pharmacist / Manager confirms the physical quantities of ordered, unexpired products that the pharmacy will keep and enters an explanation. Extra and expired products will be returned to the supplier and excluded from the GRN. The system will generate one GRN containing the confirmed kept quantities, link it to the purchase order and purchase invoice, update stock, and derive the purchase-order status from the total quantities on its linked GRNs. A later delivery will generate another GRN linked to the same purchase order and purchase invoice.
